# Ibadan is Urbn — Voice Script

Two voices: **THE SHOUTER**, a man calling from Bower's Tower, and **THE RUNNER**, someone climbing the hill to hear him.
Pronounce **"Urbn"** like **"UR-bun"** (the same as "urban"). The joke depends on it sounding like "urban".

---

## THE SHOUTER

Confident, proud, a bit theatrical. A man announcing news to the whole city.

| # | Line | Delivery | Max length |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **"Ibadan is Urbn!"** | First call. Full-chested shout across the city | ~2 s |
| S2 | **"Ibadan is Urbn!"** | Again, a little impatient. *Can't you hear me?* | ~2 s |
| S3 | **"I-BA-DAN… IS… URBN!"** | The big, clear one. Slow, punch every syllable, proud | ~2.5 s |
| S4 | **"IBADAN IS URBN!"** | Celebration, shouted together with the Runner (R6) | ~2 s |
| S5 *(optional)* | **"Ibadan is Urbn o!"** | Playful variant, ad-lib energy | ~2 s |

## THE RUNNER

Breathless and curious, then confused, then indignant, then delighted. Speaks Pidgin.

| # | Line | Delivery | Max length |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **"Oga! Wetin you dey talk?!"** | Running uphill, calling up to him, out of breath | ~2.5 s |
| R2 | **"Ibadan na… TURBO??"** | Squinting, confused, half to himself | ~2 s |
| R3 | **"Ibadan na URBAN?! Since when?!"** | Offended and proud: *Ibadan has always been a city!* Comic | ~2.5 s |
| R4 | *(panting, 4–5 s)* + **"Wait… wait o…"** | Arrived at the top, totally out of breath | ~4 s |
| R5 | **"Ahhh… Ibadan is Urbn!"** | Sees the phone. Slow realisation, then a big smile in the voice | ~2 s |
| R6 | **"IBADAN IS URBN!"** | Shouts it with the Shouter (same take as S4 if recorded together) | ~2 s |

**Wild lines (optional, 2–3 takes each):** "Ehn?", "Ah-ah!", "Huh?", a short laugh, heavy running breaths (10 s).

## How to record

- **Use any phone voice memo app.** Record in a quiet room; a wardrobe full of clothes is a great booth. Turn off fans and AC.
- **Spoken lines:** hold the phone about a hand's length (20–30 cm) from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- **Shouts:** step back to arm's length (about 60 cm) so the audio doesn't distort. Shout properly, though. **Don't** try to sound far away; I add the distance and echo in the edit.
- **Takes:** record **3 takes of every line**, with a 2-second pause before each one.
- **Voices:** record each voice separately if you can. S4/R6 can also be recorded together.
- **File names:** name files by line, e.g. `S3_take2.m4a`, `R1_take1.m4a`. Any format is fine (m4a, wav, mp3).
- **Delivery:** go a bit bigger than feels natural. It's a cartoon.
